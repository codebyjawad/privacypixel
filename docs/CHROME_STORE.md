## Detailed Description

**Stop companies from tracking your email opens.**

PrivacyPixel blocks invisible tracking pixels that companies embed in emails to spy on you. See exactly who's tracking you and take back your privacy.

**Features:**
• 🛡️ Blocks email tracking pixels automatically
• 👁️ Shows which companies are tracking you
• 📊 Real-time tracker count with badge
• 🔔 Notifications when trackers are blocked
• 📈 View tracking history
• ⚙️ Customizable settings
• 🎨 Beautiful, privacy-focused UI
• ⚡ Lightweight and fast
• 🔒 No data collection - everything stays on your device

**How It Works:**

PrivacyPixel blocks three types of email trackers:
1. Known tracking domains (Mailchimp, SendGrid, HubSpot, etc.)
2. 1x1 invisible pixel images
3. Common tracking endpoints (/track, /pixel, /open)

**Privacy First:**
- No data collection
- No external servers
- All blocking happens locally on your device
- Open source - audit the code yourself

**Perfect For:**
- Privacy-conscious users
- Anyone tired of being tracked
- People who value email privacy
- Professionals who want control
